About

Hi, I'm Alex.

I'm a software engineer based in Lisbon, currently leading the design-systems team at Northwind Software. My focus is the intersection of accessibility and frontend architecture: making sure the fast, good-looking interface and the one that works with a screen reader are always the same interface.

Outside of client work, I maintain a couple of open-source tools (see Projects), and I'm slowly getting more comfortable speaking at meetups about the unglamorous parts of frontend engineering — build tooling, migrations, and design-system maintenance.
